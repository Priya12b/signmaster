Engineering Highlights & Defense Points
  • MobileNetV2 Transfer Learning: Trained on a 5,000-image dataset (A-Z) with a 70/15/15 stratified train/val/test split completely isolated to prevent data leakage.
  • Augmentation Pipeline: Applied random rotations, color jitter (brightness, contrast, saturation), and affine translations to ensure resilience against varying camera sensors and ambient light.
  • Database Persistence: All inferences are saved to the SQLite database via `GesturePredictionLog`, supporting history auditing and performance tracking.
