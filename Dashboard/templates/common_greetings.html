Common Greetings in ASL

Everyday social interactions, polite phrases, and opening conversation signs.

Hello

Extend your dominant hand and move it from forehead in a small forward wave.

Thank You

Touch fingers of your open hand to your chin and bring your palm forward toward the person.

Good Morning

Sign 'Good' from chin into horizontal forearm, then rise your arm up like the sun rising.

Goodbye

Raise open hand with palm facing outward, flexing fingers downward in a natural farewell wave.
